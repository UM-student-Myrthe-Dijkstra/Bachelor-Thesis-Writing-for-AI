
A CV has been submitted for a job application.

Evaluate the quality of the CV in relation to the job description using the
following five criteria.

1. RELEVANCE TO THE ROLE

How well does the candidate's experience align with the job role and its
requirements?

1 = No relevance to the role.
2 = Minimal relevance to the role.
3 = Some relevance, but the alignment could be stronger.
4 = Strong relevance to the role.
5 = Excellent alignment with the job role and its requirements.

2. SIGNIFICANCE AND IMPACT

How significant or impactful is the experience described in the CV in the
context of the job being applied for?

1 = No meaningful impact; the experience is irrelevant to the job.
2 = Minimal impact; the experience has some relevance but is not significant.
3 = Moderate impact; the experience is relevant but could be more significant.
4 = Significant impact; the experience is highly relevant to the job.
5 = Extremely significant impact; the experience is central to the job.

3. SKILL RELEVANCE

How relevant are the skills described in the CV to the job description? How
applicable are those skills to the role being applied for?

1 = No relevant skills.
2 = Some skills are relevant, but they are not closely related to the role.
3 = Several skills are relevant, but important gaps remain.
4 = The skills are highly relevant and applicable to the role.
5 = The skills provide an excellent match with the job requirements.

4. ACHIEVEMENTS AND IMPACT

How impactful are the candidate's achievements or contributions in previous
roles?

1 = No notable achievements or contributions are described.
2 = Some achievements or contributions are described, but their impact is
limited.
3 = Achievements or contributions are present, but their impact is not
substantial.
4 = Significant achievements or contributions with clear evidence of results
are described.
5 = Highly impactful achievements or contributions with outstanding results
are described.

5. CULTURAL FIT AND PERSONAL TRAITS

How well do the candidate's background and personal traits fit the working
environment, values, and interpersonal expectations described in the job vacancy?

1 = No apparent cultural fit and no relevant personal traits are evident.
2 = Minimal cultural fit and only limited relevant personal traits are evident.
3 = Some cultural fit and some relevant personal traits are evident.
4 = Good cultural fit and the candidate's traits align well with the role.
5 = Excellent cultural fit and the candidate demonstrates exceptionally strong
alignment with the role.

Return only one valid JSON object containing exactly the following five keys:

relevance_to_role
significance_impact
skill_relevance
achievements_impact
cultural_fit_personal_traits

The value associated with each key must be an integer from 1 to 5.

Do not include an overall score, explanations, Markdown formatting, code
fences, comments, or additional keys.
